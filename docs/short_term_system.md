# Short-Term Trading System (Education)

This document outlines a rule-based framework. It is not investment advice.

1. Market regime
- Determine if the market is in uptrend, range, or downtrend.
- Track sentiment using limit-up breadth, consecutive limit-ups, and failure rates.

2. Limit-up framework
- First limit-up: focus on strong volume/turnover and clean breakout.
- Consecutive limit-ups: monitor opening auction strength and mid-day behavior.
- Avoid late-stage euphoria and high failure rates.

3. Pullback trend trades
- Define trend using moving averages and higher lows.
- Look for controlled pullbacks with contracting volume.

4. Oversold rebound
- Define oversold via multi-day drawdown and abnormal volatility.
- Wait for a reversal bar with volume confirmation.

5. Risk control
- Predefine invalidation conditions (price/volume rules).
- Limit single-trade loss and total daily loss.
- Keep position sizing consistent with stop distance.

6. Review loop
- Daily journaling of entries, exits, and rule violations.
- Weekly review of win/loss distribution and adverse excursion.
